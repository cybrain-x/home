PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

This Policy explains how Cosmic 2048 handles information when you play the game, enable reminders, contact support, or interact with advertising.

Effective date: July 23, 2026 · Operated by Cybrainx

1. Scope and controller 2. Information we handle 3. How information is used 4. Ads and AdMob 5. Sharing and transfers 6. Retention and security 7. Your choices and rights 8. Children 9. Updates and contact

1. Scope and controller

This Privacy Policy applies to the Cosmic 2048 mobile game, this support website, and messages that you choose to send to us. Cybrainx, based in Belo Horizonte, Minas Gerais, Brazil, is responsible for the information it directly processes under this Policy.

Cosmic 2048 does not require an account for its core gameplay. Game progress is designed to remain on your device, unless a feature clearly states otherwise.

2. Information we handle

Game data and settings stored on your device

The game stores progress and preferences locally in its private app storage. This may include scores, highest tile, achievements, statistics, completed missions, saved game state, theme, language, sound and music settings, vibration settings, and reminder choices. We do not operate an account system or cloud-save service for this local data.

Uninstalling the app, clearing its storage, or moving to another device may permanently remove local progress and settings.

Support messages you choose to send

The Contact screen prepares a message in your own email app. If you choose to send it, it may include your name, reply email address, message, selected app language, device model, operating system, screen details, and system locale. This information is sent only when you complete the send action in your email provider; Cosmic 2048 does not silently transmit a support message.

Advertising and technical information

Cosmic 2048 uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Depending on the ad request, device, region, consent status, and Google settings, that SDK may automatically collect and share an IP address, general location inferred from IP, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID or App Set ID.

We do not request precise GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone access, or a user account for normal gameplay.

Local reminders

If you enable reminders, the chosen schedule is stored on your device and used to create local notifications through the operating system. Reminder scheduling does not require us to operate a notification server or collect a cloud profile.

3. How information is used

  • Game functionality: to save progress, restore a session, apply settings, and provide missions, achievements, and statistics.
  • Reminders: to schedule or cancel local notifications that you configure.
  • Support: to read, respond to, and resolve messages you voluntarily send.
  • Advertising, analytics, and safety: to request, measure, secure, and limit fraud in ads, including through AdMob.
  • Legal and operational needs: to comply with applicable law, protect the game, and enforce our rights.

Where required by applicable law, we rely on consent, performance of the service you request, legitimate interests such as security and stability, or legal obligations. We do not sell support-message content as a separate product or use it for behavioral advertising.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

The game may show banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are optional: you decide whether to watch one in exchange for an in-game help feature. Ad availability, format, frequency, and personalization can vary by region, device, consent status, and Google settings.

  • The Google Mobile Ads SDK may use the data described above for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Data handled by the SDK is encrypted in transit by Google. Google documents its current Android SDK data practices in its Google Play data disclosure.
  • You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID and manage ad preferences through Android settings. Where a consent form is presented in the game, your choice affects ad requests as described in that form.

Google and its advertising partners process information under their own policies. For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Sharing and international transfers

We share information only as needed for the services described in this Policy. This can include Google AdMob for advertising services, Google Play and device platform services for app delivery and operation, and your email provider when you choose to send support email.

These providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where applicable, they use contractual, technical, or other safeguards required by law. We may also disclose information when necessary to comply with a legal obligation, respond to a valid request, or protect the rights, safety, and integrity of users, Cybrainx, or others.

6. Retention and security

Local game data remains on your device until you remove it, the operating system removes it, or you uninstall the app. We retain support communications only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, follow up, prevent abuse, resolve a dispute, or meet legal obligations.

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the information we directly handle. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please do not include passwords, payment details, government IDs, or other sensitive information in support messages.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Local data: you can delete local progress and settings by clearing Cosmic 2048 app data or uninstalling the app.
  • Notifications: you can turn reminders off in the app or revoke notification permission in device settings.
  • Ads: you can manage your advertising ID and relevant ad preferences in Android settings. Regional consent choices are presented when required by the applicable consent framework.
  • Support data: you may contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or information about support messages we hold, subject to applicable law and reasonable identity verification.

People in Brazil may exercise applicable rights under the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD), including confirmation of processing, access, correction, deletion or anonymization where applicable, information about sharing, consent withdrawal, and review of certain automated decisions. Rights available in other locations may vary under local law.

8. Children

Cosmic 2048 is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask a child to send personal information directly to us. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so that we can review and address the request in accordance with applicable law.

9. Updates and contact

We may update this Policy to reflect changes to the game, our providers, or applicable law. The current version and effective date will be posted on this page. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent for material changes.
